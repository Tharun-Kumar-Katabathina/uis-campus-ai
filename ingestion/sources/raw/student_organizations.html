Joining a Student Organization

The university recognizes more than 80 student organizations spanning academic, cultural, service, and recreational interests. New organizations can be chartered each semester through the Office of Student Life.

How to Join

Most organizations welcome new members at any point in the semester. Students can browse the full organization directory online, attend the involvement fair held during the first two weeks of Fall semester, or contact an organization's listed officers directly.

Starting a New Organization

To charter a new student organization, a group must submit a completed registration form, a constitution, and a list of at least five founding members to the Office of Student Life. New organization proposals are reviewed on a rolling basis.
