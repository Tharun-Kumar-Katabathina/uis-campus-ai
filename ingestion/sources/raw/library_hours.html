Brookens Library Hours

Brookens Library is open to all currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff. Regular semester hours are listed below; hours change during finals week, semester breaks, and university holidays.

Regular Semester Hours

  • Monday through Thursday: 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM
  • Friday: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Sunday: 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM

Finals Week Hours

During finals week the library extends its hours and remains open 24 hours a day from Sunday through Thursday, returning to regular hours on Friday of finals week.

Research Help

Research consultations with a subject librarian can be scheduled online or requested at the reference desk during regular hours. Chat reference is available during all open hours through the library website.
