2026-2027 Academic Calendar

This calendar lists key academic dates for the 2026-2027 academic year, including registration periods, breaks, and commencement. Dates are subject to change; always confirm with the Registrar before making travel or housing plans.

Fall 2026 Semester

Fall 2026 registration opens on July 6, 2026 for continuing students and July 20, 2026 for new and transfer students. Priority registration windows are assigned by class standing, with seniors registering first, followed by juniors, sophomores, and first-year students.

Classes begin on August 24, 2026. The add/drop period runs through September 4, 2026, after which a withdrawal grade may be recorded on a student's transcript. Labor Day, observed on September 7, 2026, is a university holiday with no classes held.

Fall Break runs from October 12 through October 13, 2026. No classes are held during this period, and most administrative offices operate on a reduced schedule. Midterm grades are due to the Registrar by October 23, 2026.

Thanksgiving Break begins November 25 and continues through November 29, 2026. Residence halls remain open, but dining services operate on a limited schedule during this period.

The last day of Fall 2026 classes is December 11, 2026. Final examinations are held December 14 through December 18, 2026. Fall commencement ceremonies take place on December 19, 2026.

Spring 2027 Semester

Spring 2027 registration opens on November 2, 2026, following the same priority order used for Fall registration. Classes begin January 19, 2027, after the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday observed on January 18, 2027.

Spring Break is scheduled for March 15 through March 19, 2027. The last day of classes is May 7, 2027, with final examinations running May 10 through May 14, 2027. Spring commencement is held May 15, 2027.

Important Deadlines

  • Graduation application deadline for Fall 2026 candidates: September 15, 2026
  • Graduation application deadline for Spring 2027 candidates: February 1, 2027
  • FAFSA priority filing deadline for 2027-2028 aid: March 1, 2027
  • Last day to withdraw from a Fall 2026 course with a "W": November 6, 2026
  • Last day to withdraw from a Spring 2027 course with a "W": April 2, 2027

Academic Year Summary

Students should note that this calendar reflects the 2026-2027 academic year specifically; prior-year and future-year calendars remain available in the Registrar's archive for reference but should not be used to plan current registration. The Office of the Registrar publishes an updated calendar each spring for the following two academic years.
