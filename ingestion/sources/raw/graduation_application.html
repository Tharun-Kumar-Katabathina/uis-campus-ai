Applying for Graduation

Students expecting to complete degree requirements must submit a graduation application through the student portal by the deadline for their intended term. Late applications may delay diploma processing and commencement participation.

Steps to Apply

  • Confirm remaining requirements with your academic advisor
  • Submit the graduation application in the student portal
  • Pay the graduation fee listed on your student account
  • Order regalia if planning to participate in the commencement ceremony

After You Apply

The Registrar's Office reviews each application against degree requirements and notifies students of any outstanding items. A final degree audit is completed after grades post for the student's final term.
